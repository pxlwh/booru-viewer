Replacing the library (Windows)
--------------------------------
booru-viewer loads libmpv-2.dll dynamically. Replace
<install directory>\libmpv-2.dll with a binary-compatible libmpv 2
and restart; no relinking or rebuilding is needed (LGPL 2.1 section 6).

Windows sources:
  mpv       https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv
  FFmpeg    https://git.ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.git
Build scripts: https://github.com/zhongfly/mpv-winbuild
